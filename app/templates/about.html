About Our Medical AI System

Revolutionizing Early Cancer Detection & Risk Prediction across Erode District

Clinical Mission

To provide accessible, accurate, and early cancer detection services to the people of Erode District using cutting-edge artificial intelligence.

We believe early diagnosis saves lives. Our hospital AI platform evaluates medical imaging (MRI, CT scans, Chest X-rays, Dermoscopy) and clinical risk factors to detect cancer at early treatable stages, empowering clinicians and patients.

Key Clinical Objectives

  • Free screening for community patients in Erode District
  • 95%+ Multi-Modal Diagnostic Accuracy
  • Real-time Grad-CAM Heatmap Explainability
  • Instant Automated PDF Medical Diagnostic Reports
  • Integrated Doctor-Patient Appointment Scheduling
