About Our Project

Revolutionizing Cancer Detection with Artificial Intelligence

Our Mission

To provide accessible, accurate, and early cancer detection services to the people of Erode District using cutting-edge artificial intelligence technology.

We believe that early detection saves lives. Our AI-powered system analyzes medical imaging and patient data to identify potential cancer risks at their earliest stages, enabling timely intervention and improved treatment outcomes.

Key Objectives

  • Provide free cancer screening services
  • Achieve 95%+ detection accuracy
  • Reduce diagnosis time by 50%
  • Serve underserved communities in Erode District
  • Integrate with local healthcare infrastructure
